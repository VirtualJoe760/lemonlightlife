# Seeding — 10,000 subcontractors

The brief mandates 10k people covering the full range of construction roles.
For this MVP the geographic scope is **Southern California** — LA, Orange,
Riverside (incl. Coachella Valley), San Bernardino, San Diego, and Imperial
counties. Ten thousand people in that region is realistic density and lets
the geo scoring signal actually matter (a Palm Desert electrician should
rank higher than a Long Beach one for a Palm Desert job).

## Scripts

- `server/scripts/generate-headshots.js` — one-time. Generates ~40 diverse
  construction-worker headshots via Google Imagen 3 (falls back to Gemini
  2.5 Flash Image). Saves to `client/public/headshots/*.png` + a
  `manifest.json` the seeder reads.
- `server/scripts/seed.js` — reruns whenever. Generates 10,000 subcontractor
  records, assigns headshots deterministically from the manifest, and
  ensures indexes.

## Distributions

### Role coverage (weighted)

Weighted by real-world construction workforce composition:

| Role bucket | Weight |
|---|---|
| carpenter / framer | 15 |
| electrician | 12 |
| plumber | 10 |
| general laborer | 10 |
| painter | 8 |
| drywall | 7 |
| hvac | 7 |
| roofer | 6 |
| flooring | 5 |
| mason, concrete, tile | 4 each |
| supervisor | 4 |
| specialty (welder, glazier, cabinet, waterproofing, foundation, siding, insulation, demolition, solar, gutter, landscaper, excavator) | 1–3 each |

Roughly 20% of subcontractors get a **second role** to model overlapping
skills (a carpenter who also frames; a supervisor who welds).

### Specializations (the interesting part)

Each subcontractor gets **3–6 specializations** sampled from
`SKILL_POOL_BY_ROLE[role]` for whichever role(s) they hold.

Each specialization has a `level` (1–5) drawn from this distribution:

| Level | Meaning | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Expert / go-to | 15% |
| 4 | Strong | 25% |
| 3 | Proficient | 35% |
| 2 | Familiar | 20% |
| 1 | Novice / helper | 5% |

And `yearsInSpecialty` — always ≤ overall `yearsExperience`, sampled to be
correlated with `level` (level-5 specialists average more years in that
specialty than level-2 dabblers).

This is the signal the ranker uses to distinguish "a flooring guy who does
vinyl" from "a flooring guy who's done a thousand vinyl jobs."

### Geographic distribution

`shared/cities.js` lists ~85 SoCal cities each tagged with a
population-proxy weight. Seeder picks a city weighted by that number, then
jitters lat/lng by ±0.15° so map markers spread across the city instead of
stacking on the centroid.

Result: heaviest density in LA, San Diego, Orange County; realistic
coverage of Coachella Valley (Palm Springs, Indio, La Quinta, etc.); sparse
but present coverage of Imperial Valley and mountain towns.

### Availability

| Status | Share |
|---|---|
| `available` | 70% |
| `booked` | 20% |
| `unavailable` | 10% |

### Experience & rating

- `yearsExperience`: 1–35, skewed toward 5–15.
- `rating`: 1.0–5.0, skewed toward 3.8–4.6.

### Gender

50/50 male/female split (Faker's gendered name generation ensures the name
matches). Used to filter which headshot the subcontractor is assigned.

### Headshot assignment

The seeder reads `client/public/headshots/manifest.json` produced by
`generate-headshots.js`. For each subcontractor:

1. Filter manifest to `{ role: primaryRole, gender: subcontractor.gender }`.
   Fall back to matching gender only if no role match. Fall back to any
   headshot if no gender match.
2. Pick deterministically: `manifest[hash(_id) % candidates.length]`.

Same person, same photo across runs. Rare edge case (rare role + gender
combo has no headshot) is handled by the double-fallback.

### Certifications

40% of subcontractors have at least one certification, sampled from the
pool in `shared/roles.js`. Higher for electricians (journeyman/master),
roofers (OSHA 30), solar installers (NABCEP).

### Bio

Generated by concatenating templated phrases keyed off role and top
specializations, plus a Faker sentence for flavor:

> "Marcus is a residential electrician based in Palm Desert with 12 years
> on the tools. Specializes in kitchen rewires and panel upgrades — a
> go-to for kitchen rewires (level 5). Journeyman licensed. Reliable
> and communicates well."

The bio is the free-text field Atlas Search leans on for fuzzy queries.

## Performance

Batched `insertMany` of 1,000 completes 10k in ~15–30s on a free M0 tier.
Index creation adds a few seconds.

## Determinism

Faker's seed is fixed at the top of `seed.js`. Re-running `npm run seed`
produces the same 10k records (modulo Mongo `_id` values). Matters for
demo — reproducible test queries.
